# Landing — overrides (Veluxa)

> Overrides `design-system/veluxa-landing/MASTER.md` for the public marketing page.

## Direction

- **Style:** Exaggerated minimalism + solemn luxury (editorial), not AI SaaS.
- **Mode:** Dark ink plane only (`#0C0A09` / `#141210`). Do not use light cream marketing.
- **Accent:** Brand gold `#E7C27A` / `#C4A574` — never pink, purple, neon, or glow blobs.
- **Type:** Cormorant (display) + Source Sans 3 (body). Avoid Inter + Playfair defaults.
- **Hero budget:** Brand mark + name, one headline, one sentence, one CTA group. No stats, badges, or cards in the hero.
- **Visual anchor:** Oversized brand mark on a full-bleed charcoal field with subtle film grain (no stock collage).
- **Motion:** Enter stagger 150–300ms; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Anti-patterns:** Cream/beige wash, frosted glass, pill clusters, purple gradients, “glow” radials, SaaS pricing cards with shadows.
